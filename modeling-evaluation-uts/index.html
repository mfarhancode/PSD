Modeling and Evaluation

Model¶

Random Forest (scikit-learn): 300 trees, balanced class weights, random seed 42. Input features: B04, B8A, B11, NDVI, NDMI. Code: 03_extract_and_train.py.

Mangrove and forest have almost the same red reflectance (B04), but mangrove has higher B8A and lower B11 than forest. This difference lets the model separate them: only 18 of 1,031 mangrove test pixels were predicted as forest, and 9 of 4,465 forest test pixels as mangrove.

Improving the model¶

  • The first run kept only polygons of at least 2,000 m², which left 15 farmland polygons. Lowering the minimum to 500 m² kept 41 farmland polygons (237 polygons in total).

Limitations¶

  • Farmland is the weakest class (F1 0.225). Its polygons are small, so it has only 438 training and test pixels against 14,965 for forest, and only 43 farmland pixels in the test set. Most farmland test pixels (27 of 43) were predicted as water, and 74 water pixels were predicted as farmland; flooded rice fields, which look like water, are a likely cause. The overall accuracy is dominated by the large classes and says little about farmland.

  • Because of this, the class areas in the app, especially farmland, built-up and forest, should be read as estimates rather than exact figures.

  • The map covers the 12 downloaded tiles, not the whole province.

  • The next step to improve the result would be more and larger farmland polygons.
