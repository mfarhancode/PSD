Data Understanding

Study area¶

East Java province, Indonesia. Boundary: geoBoundaries (ADM1, simplified), clipped to the province. Far offshore islands (Kangean, Masalembu, Bawean) are left out to save download quota. Sentinel-2 data was downloaded for the 12 tiles (50 km x 50 km) that contain the sample polygons.

Satellite data¶

  • Sentinel-2 L2A (Copernicus Data Space, via openEO), 1 January to 31 December 2025.

  • Bands used, as asked in the brief (8A-11-4):

BandNameNative resolution
B8ANear infrared (narrow)20 m
B11Short-wave infrared20 m
B04Red10 m

  • The data is processed at 20 m in UTM zone 49S (EPSG:32749).

Sample polygons¶

Polygons were digitized by hand in geojson.io, each with a property holding its class.

The original labels were mapped to the five class names:

Original labelClass (kelas)
Pemukimanbuilt_up
Sawahfarmland
Airwater
Hutan non mangroveforest
Hutan mangrovemangrove

248 polygons were digitized. 01_prepare_samples.py drops polygons that are too small to contain pixels. With a minimum area of 2,000 m² only 204 polygons remained, and farmland kept just 15 polygons. Because rice fields are small, the minimum was lowered to 500 m², which dropped 11 polygons and kept 237, including 41 farmland polygons.

Known limitations of the samples¶

  • Some polygons are large (16 are larger than 5 km², for example sea and forest) and may contain mixed land cover. At most 300 random pixels are used per polygon so large polygons do not dominate.

  • Farmland polygons are small (median 1,306 m²), so they contribute few pixels.

  • Fishponds next to mangrove and flooded rice fields can look like water.
